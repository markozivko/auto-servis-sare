Do you only service Opel cars?

Opel is our speciality. We also offer express servicing and parts supply for other makes. Tell us your car model and the service you need so we can agree the details.

How can I book an appointment?

Call 01 3700 174 on weekdays from 08:00 to 16:00 or send us an email. Your appointment is confirmed once agreed with the garage.

How much will my service cost?

The price depends on the vehicle, parts needed and work involved. For an estimate, send us your car’s make and model and a description of the service you need.

Where are you and when are you open?

You’ll find us at Črnomerec 35 in Zagreb, near the ZET transport terminal. We are open Monday to Friday, from 08:00 to 16:00. We are closed on Saturdays and Sundays.
